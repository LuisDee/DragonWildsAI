## How a pass is measured

- **Recording:** the dev server's read-only recorder (mod/experiments/player_recorder.lua, armed via debug_run) logs
  5 Hz pose, inventory deltas and action RPCs for every player. Re-arm it after a server restart; files go to
  logs/recorder/rec_*.jsonl.
- **Per-task bars:** evals/baselines/luis_20261101_tasks.json (tools/baseline_tasks.py). Per-unit bars:
  data/baselines/luis-2026-11-01.json.
- **Grades:** tools/standard_score.py (H = within 1.5x Luis, A = done but slower). Co-play gate: tools/coplay_ladder.py.
  Per-request timelines and idle causes: tools/session_report.py.
- **Without Luis:** tools/luis_emulator.py (replay his lines at $0; a Sonnet writer behind --sonnet).
