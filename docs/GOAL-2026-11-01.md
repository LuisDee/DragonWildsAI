**What counts as a miss** (grader rule, main 2026-11-02; tools/rung1_gate.py count_fumbles, used by the rung3/rung4/farm gates):
- A miss is a world action that failed, or an action collision (still_running).
- Not a miss: an interruption.
- Not a miss: a read-only lookup (craft_plan, recipes, inventory, look, stations, buildables, ...) that answers ambiguous_target or not-found. It changes nothing, and its time is already in the time bar.

## How the work is run (main, 2026-11-16, after the 17-hour run; docs/RETRO-2026-11-16-workflow.md)

The gate does not change: an item passes when it is done live 3 runs in a row inside every bar. What changes is how the work gets there, because there is one live body and a whole chain costs 15 to 26 minutes of it.

- **Working metric: the pass rate of the item alone.** K short trials from a checkpoint in one window (`tools.quest_run step --from <checkpoint> --quest "<name>" -k 5`), read with `tools.quest_run rates`. A trial from a checkpoint is its own item ("Rune Mysteries @ rm_part2") and never counts toward the gate's streak, which is for the item from its start.
- **Gate last.** Try the 3 in a row only when the item passes about 9 of 10 alone. At 9 of 10 a streak of three takes about 4 trials on average; at 3 of 13 (Rune Mysteries on 2026-11-16) about 104. `quest_run fresh` refuses the chain until every quest in it has passed 4 of its last 5 trials; `--chain-anyway REASON` overrides and is recorded.
- **Void runs.** A run that failed through the harness (the run or the grader, never something he did) is tagged `--void` in the ledger with its cause. It is rerun and resets no streak. Every void run stays listed in `docs/failure_ledger.jsonl` for Luis to check.
- **Ledger before fix.** `tools.failure_ledger scan`, one tag per failed run for the first thing that went wrong (model, skill, harness, game), and the fix goes to the cause with the most open runs.
- **Visibility before a second fix.** If the game's own state for the item cannot be read (quest flags, station contents), make it readable before fixing again.
- **Search at the triggers.** Ten minutes at the first failure of a new class and after two fixes that did not move the rate; the game's wiki for the game's rules.
- **Three live iterations without movement:** stop, search, change the approach.
