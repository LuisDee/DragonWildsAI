# Forward floor integrity (2026-10-27)

## Goal and evidence

Before ordinary ground steering moves the companion into a surface transition, use current, bounded evidence of support and a traversable next edge. If support cannot be established, stop input and re-observe or plan; report the specific outcome if progress cannot continue. The model still chooses the destination. This is a proposed body capability, not a claim that a particular fall would have been prevented.

The a124 incident has a verified rapid descent and KillZ death; the departure geometry and cause were not observed. The client logged local and remembered-exit segments, then `src=straight` for the last reported 24 s. The separate 2026-10-27 22:19:02 dev observation found a downward hit at Z -3071.73 cm while the pawn centre was -2980.76 cm and capsule half-height 88 cm; `MaxStepHeight=45` cm. The trace's `bStartPenetrating` was true despite that normal-looking floor hit. Eight knee rays using the `Custom` profile had no hits; earlier project evidence says `Custom` is not a usable collision preset. Neither observation verifies terrain at the fatal location. Do not infer a cliff from a missing hit or from the penetration bit.

## Existing code and reuse boundary

`move.lua` selects straight, remembered, oracle and local sources, then sends player input through one `AddMovementInput` point. `raw_trace` and `make_trace` already provide client collision line traces with validated `Player`/`Pawn` profile selection and Visibility fallback. `localplan.lua` `cell_floors` samples two floor layers and `LP:edge` classifies adjacent sampled nodes using step, slope, jump and drop envelope; `LP.prefetch` fills a 30 s cache. These are **not directly a support verifier**: `cell_floors` is private, `LP.prefetch` returns trace count, not the sample; empty cells conflate no hit/invalid trace and the cache may be stale, and `LP:edge` expects a planner with node indices, `hop`/`steep` verification and environmental configuration. `make_trace` reduces a hit to Z and `Distance < 0.5`, not the capsule footprint, and `raw_trace` can fall back between collision channels. A line hit is not a capsule sweep or proof of safe landing. Do not read `bStartPenetrating` as a reliable ground truth. Existing prefetch may aid planning, but cannot alone authorize an edge. Reuse its trace and floor-layer algorithms only after exposing a bounded result with explicit read status, profile, age, hit distance, normal and layer; adapt `LP:edge` only for measured adjacent samples and preserve its lazy segment verification. No new native engine function or engine-property write is assumed.

## Measurement first

Before implementing any behavior guard or choosing a threshold, root schedules brief, read-only observations on the existing dev client under its sole operator; no telemetry here acts on the client. Sample safe, stable flat ground and a known low step/benign drop, at rest and during separately authorized ordinary movement. Record timestamp, world, pawn/capsule dimensions and foot height, ground/movement mode, native velocity if already verified available (otherwise measured displacement and mark native velocity unavailable), movement source, intended direction, trace profile and raw hit/impact Z/normal/distance plus both reported penetration flag and the distance-based interpretation, exact offsets, sample ages, latency and trace count. Contrast direct nearby samples with planner-prefetch cells and show that gear, layer choice, water and `Custom`/Visibility fallback do not cause false support claims. Record tick rate and game-thread cost; enforce a small fixed trace/time budget and stop on uncertainty or resource pressure. Flat observation is not an edge calibration; the 55 cm floor-spread jump calibration correctly refused its 30 cm limit and proves neither a jump nor a guard threshold.

On safe flat terrain, measure displacement after the existing player input is released at representative walking and sprinting speeds, in repeated bounded trials owned by the operator. Reconcile lag, jitter, turns, downhill movement and any already-queued input with stopping distance. A formula or safety distance cannot be asserted from the incident's 6.8 m/s alone. Gather controlled fixtures for flat/slope/stairs, shallow water and a benign step before agreeing any limits. A missing floor on an unloaded cell or line occluded by equipment is **unknown**, not a verified drop. Moving-platform support requires fresh observations; stale world-aligned cached data cannot certify it. The sole operator retains the slot; queue only after the current task yields, with dev/forbidden-target guards and measured budget. Record results without clearing or migrating per-world memory or envelopes.

## Proposed contract, subject to measurements and review

At the common steering boundary, classify a short forward support corridor for **all** ordinary ground steering sources, including `straight`, `memory`, oracle waypoints and local walking segments; do not trust a route merely because its source was once marked successful. Use a fresh current-floor reference, capsule dimensions, actual direction and speed, and a bounded series of offsets spanning the measured lookahead and relevant footprint. Compare successive valid floor layers with the same step/slope/drop semantics as the planner and verify any candidate edge with existing wall/edge logic. The sampling geometry, freshness window, margin and cadence must be selected from the observations, not fixed here. An adequate lookahead must cover input reaction and measured stopping displacement; if the available collision range, sampling budget or data freshness cannot establish this, classify as unknown. A single downward ray or sparse cache entry is insufficient to assert footprint support.

Outcomes: **supported** only when fresh observations and calibrated rules establish the ordinary next segment; **known non-traversable** only when valid observations actually show a transition beyond measured envelope; **unknown** for failed/ambiguous read, unsupported collision profile, no return, unloaded range, insufficient lookahead, stale data, invalid hit or an unvalidated platform/water case. For known non-traversable or unknown, stop issuing ordinary movement input, avoid treating prefetch as a successful edge, and use the existing bounded local replanner where evidence permits. If it cannot establish a route, finish `go_to` with a truthful `path_blocked` or `verification_failed` detail; persistent `follow` remains active in its existing unreachable/retry state and emits the existing event, rather than silently ending. Keep reason distinct: “floor could not be verified” is not “cliff.” Pausing input is not guaranteed to halt momentum; no rollout as a prevention fix until stopping-distance validation demonstrates the available margin.

Preserve explicit segment semantics. A locally verified `jump`, `djump`, `superjump`/Windstep or bounded `drop` edge has its own take-off, landing and executor; validate its planned landing and execution state rather than demanding continuous ground under its airborne interval. Revalidate take-off and landing as applicable, preserve `ws_exec`, `ws_job`, timer generation and arbiter token ownership, and let existing `finish`/`cancel_superjump` and `release_implicit` handle failure exactly once. Do not suppress a deliberate jump because the gap is unsupported; do not turn an unplanned gap into a jump. Treat water using existing `wade`, `in_water` and separately calibrated water sense; no interpretation of an invisible water surface as a confirmed lethal void. For stairs, slopes and near step boundaries, use calibrated tolerances and existing verified edge behavior rather than an arbitrary raw height cutoff. For moving platforms, await fresh support data or replan, rather than replaying stale cache. Preserve escape, reflex suspension, follow retry and target movement semantics; no new LLM veto or entity/area special case.

## Principles and acceptance

Generic across sources, targets and worlds; user input remains through native player paths. Preserve navigation/trap data, including r96, rather than blacklisting or erasing it. Outcome is evidence-based, with a specific failure and no synthetic motion or state. Files likely required after review: `move.lua` and a small shared/exported helper in `localplan.lua`, with targeted `test_move.lua` / local planner tests; a bounded queue diagnostic may require a separately owned command/check. No judge files. No behavior change until fresh-context review and measurement.

Offline regression matrix after a calibrated design: supported flat and undulating slopes; safe versus excessive step/drop boundaries; no hit, trace error, unreliable penetration bit, invalid profile, attached gear and stale cache; unloaded cells and fresh/stale moving platform; water/wade; near and far lookahead at different speeds and delayed braking; each steering source; partial planner route; planned jump/double-jump/Windstep/drop with landing verification; interruption, owner loss, supersession and cancellation once; persistent follow failure/retry and truthful go_to detail. Verify normal progress and meaningful no-fall negative controls, not implementation mirrors. A live queue evaluation on current deployed code must first record flat, known safe step/drop and release-to-stop evidence, then validate the candidate on non-dangerous held-out terrain with independent position/mode/health observation and report throughput/false blocks. Do not use the fatal destination as a live fixture; an observational check does not count as prevention proof.
