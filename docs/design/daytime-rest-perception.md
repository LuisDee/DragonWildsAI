# Daytime rest perception (2026-10-27)

## Goal and evidence

Give the model a precise account of the game's sleep-window signal, visible interaction prompts and observed rest state, without claiming a daytime effect that this dev character has not demonstrated. The model remains free to choose its own actions.

The 22:10–22:11 UTC self-care trace (`C:/gameserver/logs/brain-live-20261027-231004-4340.jsonl`) shows endurance 0, health 1, food 0. It chose water, searched/attempted food, walked and attempted a spell; no rest action was recorded before the operator stopped the task. The handoff records hydration improved but food and rest did not. This does **not** prove why it chose those actions, that it consciously deferred rest, or that confusing wording caused its decision.

The actual prompts contained two different lines: `brain/situation.py` rendered `too early to sleep` and `endurance, restored by sleep`; `brain/needs.py` also rendered `too early to sleep (resting still restores)`. Thus the model was not simply missing a daytime-rest hint: the wording was ambiguous and one line asserted an unmeasured outcome. `sense.time_of_day` maps `PlayerRestComponent:IsInTimeframeForSleep()` to `sleep_allowed`; it does not ask whether a resting area is usable or whether endurance is increasing. A false result is evidence that this **sleep timeframe** is closed, not evidence that every kind of rest is unavailable. Nil/failed reads are unknown. The client census already reports interactables and their actual prompt; `needs._rest` presents nearby bed/resting-area prompts and ownership where available. Generic `interact` uses the game detector and player input; `CurrentRestingAreaActor`/`resting_in` supplies observed lying state and the same interaction can leave it. Current `press_signals` reads `sleeping`, but `IsSleeping=false` is not proof of not resting: prior night interaction observed a resting area while `IsSleeping` was false after the night transition.

The project's read-only historical `rest_probe.lua` observed a game-provided text describing a daytime nap and recovery, and the summoned shelter prompt changed from daytime `Rest` to nighttime `Sleep` (DISCOVERIES). This is game-authored **intent/UI evidence**, not a measured endurance increase in this dev run. A prior real-client night interaction established a resting area and Luis saw the day transition; it did not establish this daytime recovery rate. Older server-puppet direct Sleep experiments are historical context only and do not establish a permitted current-client action. The search result for the community wiki describes daytime recharge, but the wiki page could not be opened due to robots restrictions and is secondary/version-sensitive; do not promote it to a verified current-build fact.

## Minimal proposed change

After fresh review, adjust the existing two renderers `brain/situation.py` and `brain/needs.py` and their targeted tests. Render the boolean as the **game's sleep timeframe open/closed**, without asserting that a player can actually sleep or that it is too early for all rest. Retain separately the numeric endurance observation, current resting-area state and actual nearby object prompts/availability exactly as supplied. Remove categorical `resting still restores` / `restored by sleep` from the snapshot until there is current-build outcome evidence; if game-authored rest/nap text is already exposed as sourced data, report it as such, not as observed outcome. Do not fabricate a new flag, require a particular object, infer a bed from intent, force a skill, or call unverified reflected methods. Do not treat the `sleep_allowed` boolean as `CanRest`; a true timeframe does not guarantee sleep is actually possible. Unknown/missing data stays unknown and is never turned into false or an action recommendation. This narrow change prevents internally inconsistent claims; it does not solve navigation, food, nighttime multiplayer rules or the model's choice.

If developer validation confirms a daytime recovery effect, record that mechanic with build, conditions and provenance before exposing it through the existing knowledge/perception path. Exact UI text may describe intent; a fresh increase in authoritative EnduranceComponent values while the game observes resting is outcome evidence. Do not add a hard-coded recovery rate from a third-party page. No preselected plan or phrase-to-action rule is delivered to the model.
