# Navigation outcome integrity (2026-10-27)

## Goal

A movement result says arrived only when a fresh, living player character is actually at the requested destination's height and within its horizontal tolerance. Death, replacement of the controlled pawn, missing verification, or an unreachable floor produce a specific failure. Do not erase or rewrite learned per-world locations.

## Approach for review

Reuse existing movement, `HealthComponent:IsDead()` / authoritative-health read pattern and `CharacterMovement:IsMovingOnGround()`; do not introduce NPC control or writes. Evaluate native reads on the current controlled character at start, before movement and terminal completion. Capture the starting pawn and a life-generation/observed-death signal where available: a death then revive may reuse the same pawn, so identity alone does not establish continuity. When life continuity is lost, terminate once with `companion_down` / a truthful interruption; cancel Windstep job and delayed action, release only the owned arbiter token, and do not mark the trip successful. Unknown or failed reads are not evidence of being alive or grounded; report inability to verify rather than invent death. Respect the existing world-teardown pause guard before native reads, and bound any pending verification so it cannot remain silently active.

Keep the immutable original goal (or freshly resolved moving actor goal), separate from oracle projections, intermediate waypoints and partial segments. For ordinary point/actor go_to, require fresh horizontal distance within the effective requested tolerance and a separately calibrated vertical tolerance consistent with pawn-centre vs floor/actor-origin semantics. Require stable grounded confirmation where appropriate; falling, jumping and Windstep execution must not report arrival merely while passing through the radius. Do not assume every target is a floor: unsupported, missing or ambiguous vertical coordinates must yield a specific unverifiable outcome, not fabricate `Z=0`. Maintain existing escape-zone semantics as a distinct success predicate, not an ordinary destination. At bridge completion independently re-read current pawn, position, alive/ground state and original target; missing data or a mismatch fails verification, with actual distances and requested target. For moving actors, resolve and verify their current position, not stale initial coordinates. Follow remains persistent and must not silently stop; the same life interruption reports a specific failure.

The suspected fall path needs a separate generic check: inspect measured forward collision/floor traces and their failure modes before choosing an edge guard. If straight paths can move into unknown or excessive drops, reuse local planner's validated floor/edge logic to stop or replan when a forward footprint has no confirmed traversable floor, accounting for intended jumps, water, moving geometry and latency. Unknown is not automatically a cliff; surface a blocked/unverifiable result when safety cannot be established. Instrument position, movement mode, floor readings, original/projected goal and death signal in a bounded dev observation to establish the trajectory. Arrival checks alone cannot prevent a fall, and the death may occur before the replicated health signal arrives. This candidate requires its own grounded review and tests, not a grave-specific taboo.

## Candidate status

Candidate uses current pawn identity and `DW_LIFE.move_death_seq`, incremented when the existing bridge consumes a death event. That sequence is an observed event, not a new native hook: a death and revival entirely between polls before the bridge consumes the event cannot be excluded. `IsDead`, authoritative health, capsule half-height and `IsMovingOnGround` are checked natively; read failures fail verification. The provisional vertical window is one measured capsule's full height, derived from the known pawn-centre versus floor offset, and requires live calibration for actor origins and ordinary uneven terrain. Closer stacked floors can overlap the window; the result reports vertical difference and tolerance instead of claiming a spherical distance or proof of floor identity. Movement checks the original goal, and bridge completion compares it with the requested coordinates and a second fresh position. The existing held-follow action path sends one `task_failed` event when the move ends and clears following; the broker retains that event. The candidate puts the observed terminal detail in its existing brain-facing reason field instead of adding a duplicate notification. Offline movement and bridge suites pass, including existing Windstep tests and a death during its pending cast. Live/native calibration, fresh-context code review and the full regression gate remain outstanding. The forward-floor hypothesis remains separate and unmeasured.
