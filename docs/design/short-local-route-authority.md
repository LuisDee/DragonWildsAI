# Short local route authority

## Goal and evidence

Allow an owned, short original goal to use a complete local-planner route when the existing route guard requires authority for a height difference and the oracle is pending or cannot supply a complete route. Long trips continue to require the oracle. The current native observation returned a typed route without moving the pawn, and its endpoint was 0.539 m from the coordinate supplied to the diagnostic, which did not specify a movement tolerance. That endpoint would miss a subsequent 0.5 m tolerance but fits the default 1.5 m tolerance. Its line traces, measured jump apex and observed route are not proof of collision clearance or a safe landing.

## Approach

In `move.lua`, adapt the existing local planner to observe the **original** goal while required-route input remains held. Eligibility requires original XY distance within both the existing oracle-primary boundary and local-planner grid; no `nav=false` override. Sampling, path verification and failure must not publish an active path, initiate movement, update navigation/trap outcomes, or cancel/clear the pending oracle operation. A local candidate becomes authority only after a complete endpoint meets the original requested XY tolerance and the existing level tolerance, explicitly converting planner floor height and pawn/goal centre height with the measured capsule half-height. Actor targets supply `K2_GetActorLocation()` while coordinate targets preserve their supplied Z; the existing `reached()` compares the pawn centre with that goal using its existing vertical tolerance. Retain the final admitted waypoint until actual `reached()` succeeds: the ordinary local waypoint radius can exceed the requested final tolerance. Nearest-node, wrong-level, unknown, stale, or outside-tolerance results are not complete; do not inflate tolerance to admit the observed diagnostic path.

Bind an admitted route and its original goal to the current move state/generation, exact owner record and token, controller/pawn/life, world observation, and relevant goal/capsule values. Reuse the existing route binding and post-reentrant checks before each input; invalidate on pause, supersession, identity or goal change. Once selected, an admitted local route authorizes only its own waypoints, never raw-goal, memory or trail fallback. Keep oracle single-flight bookkeeping independent and define deterministic route selection without mixing waypoints. Unknown or exhausted authority uses existing truthful failure/persistent-follow handling rather than recording an obstruction or success. Reuse existing bounds and freshness policies; no new speculative terrain threshold.

For ordinary typed jump and double jump, adapt existing owned jump and Windstep landing discipline: known grounded takeoff, actual accepted input, observed airborne and then known grounded at that edge's endpoint and level under the same binding before advancement or success learning. Unknown, missed landing, cancellation, or changed owner cannot advance, record success or affect a successor. Existing measured/perk gates and real player input remain in force. This transaction is not a capsule-trajectory certificate; other local-planner limitations remain explicit.

## Acceptance and principles

Actual-module fakes cover input held during planning, admitted full path, partial/nearest endpoint, wrong height and exact requested tolerance including a 0.539 m versus 0.5 m boundary; long-goal exclusion; pending oracle and late response; pawn/life/world/goal/owner changes, pause and reentrant replacement; jump/double-jump takeoff, air, landing, missing/incorrect landing and no false learning; normal flat movement, persistent follow, Windstep and cancellation. A fresh code review and full regression gate precede developer-driven live verification of selection, real input, air, landing, health and cleanup, then a bounded model run. Preserve player input, model intent, per-world memories and judge definitions; no item/location rule or invented model plan.
