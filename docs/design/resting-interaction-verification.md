# Verify resting after an interaction (2026-10-28)

## Goal and evidence

Independent review approved this design with a prerequisite: establish reliable known-nonresting evidence on the installed runtime. A getter name or prior observational probe alone is insufficient. The proposed property-only route below avoids introducing an `IsResting` call, but requires fresh validation before implementation; failed or unavailable reads remain unknown. This narrower known-nonresting baseline supersedes the older audit's different/empty wording.

An interaction that has established a new resting state at its actual selected target should report `resting`, not continue trying to walk and eventually report `path_blocked`. In the developer-directed dev case on `cde20004`, the log recorded one `IA_Interact`, later movement retries and a stuck failure. Post-action native state reported `IsResting=true`, `CurrentRestingAreaActor` equal to the requested shelter, `IsSleeping=false`, and `DIM_Resting`; endurance rose from 0 to 33 and then 54. The setup included a developer-controlled time change and a real shelter spell using carried runes. It was not an autonomous success. The first press preceded the observed resting state, but neither its exact transition time nor its cause was captured; probe getter names alone do not prove side-effect freedom. No claim of nighttime sleep follows from daytime rest.

The deployed episode captures `W.press_signals` before use but retains only `sleeping`, not `resting_in`; it recognizes same-target rest only for the separate initial leave-rest action. After verification times out it can re-approach, while its approach check rejects only a *different* resting actor, eventually failing the no-progress watchdog. This is a source-consistent explanation, not a complete trace of intermediate reads. Current `528ae99` is separately tested and published; R23 screen handling does not establish rest-transition causality.

## Approach

Reuse the existing interaction episode and reflected `PlayerRestComponent.CurrentRestingAreaActor` property. Candidate typed observation: **known empty area**, **known valid area actor**, or **unknown**, with protected-read success tracked separately from values. The local SDK declares this as `AActor*` at component offset `0x1D0`; the existing `W.resting_in` and `sense.time_of_day` helpers collapse missing component, failed reads and empty actor to nil, so nil alone is never a known empty area. After confirming the expected local pawn and valid component, use protected property access followed by protected `GetAddress`: zero may represent a known empty pointer only after the installed runtime's property marshalling is independently verified; nonzero plus a valid named actor can represent a known area; nil/no wrapper, error, missing/noninteger address, or nonzero-invalid wrapper stays unknown. Never infer zero from `IsValid=false`. `IsSleeping` stays a separate fact and is never inferred from the area.

This is a proposed property-only path, **not yet proof** that an empty area is always equivalent to nonresting. Existing developer observations show absent area with `IsResting=false` before the interaction, a matching area and `IsResting=true` afterward, and a later ordinary same-bed exit with native resting=false and gameplay mode. They do not record raw null pointer representation or establish universal equivalence. The single live operator should first compare the protected wrapper's `GetAddress` and validity with a read-only debugger's pointer value at `0x1D0`, for a confirmed nonresting state and separately an observed valid resting actor when available, retaining stable pawn/component identity and before/after state. A zero, pointer representation or association not established by those measurements remains unknown; do not move or alter the character just to manufacture evidence. Existing probe calls such as `IsResting` were previously observed but are not introduced into the production path. If the property-only baseline remains unproven, defer the fix instead of assuming it or adding a getter call.

Immediately before an accepted real interact press, capture a known nonresting baseline, the actual detector-selected actor (including an accepted alternate), and stable pawn/arbiter ownership. A later known transition to resting at that **same selected actor**, with unchanged pawn and relevant ownership, can acknowledge the interaction as `resting`; report the observed actor and actual selected target, not an inferred unlock or night sleep. Check during the verify phase and again before retrying, declaring movement failure, or starting further approach work, so a late observation is not missed. If navigation is still active, stop it only through the existing owned-token path; never stop another owner. Baseline unknown, pre-existing rest, inconsistent pointer, wrapper or actor, a different actor, a changed pawn or lost ownership cannot prove the transition. Report the specific uncertainty or different-resting obstruction without suppressing a real failure; never press again while the observed state says resting. Preserve the existing same-actor-at-entry leave-rest behavior as a distinct request, including its own verified exit.

Pause and suspension invalidate the transaction's correlation; do no native calls under pause and do not credit an event while another owner controlled the pawn. On resume, do not silently re-baseline a prior press as a new success or send another press while already resting; surface a truthful uncertain/interrupted result unless a new explicitly accepted interaction is independently performed. Cancellation, timeout and failed reads retain R23's scoped screen cleanup and do not leak claims. These are evidence/ownership rules, not category, name, phrase or benchmark-specific mappings; no additional movement or monitoring framework and no new native ABI are proposed.

This avoids adding an unverified native `IsResting` call; it does not assert that the property's null value alone universally proves nonresting.

Preserve single voice, dev-only guards, one client/operator, world memory and frozen judges. No principle exception requested. A fresh-context review is required before implementation.

Offline fake-engine tests must cover an established empty-area baseline to same-target daytime resting with sleeping=false after one accepted press; a late transition before retry and during owned movement failure; explicit unknown and inconsistent pointer/wrapper readings; pre-existing rest, other actor, accepted alternate detector, changed pawn, pause, cancel, suspension and another owner.
