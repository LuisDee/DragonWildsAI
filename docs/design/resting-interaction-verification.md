# Verify resting after an interaction (2026-10-28)

## Goal and evidence

Independent review approved this design with a prerequisite: establish the IsResting getter's actual ABI and read-only contract on the current runtime before adding a production call. A getter name or prior observational probe alone is insufficient. If that cannot be established, observations remain unknown; never convert failed/unavailable reads into a nonresting baseline. This design's narrower known-nonresting baseline supersedes the older audit's different/empty wording.

An interaction that has established a new resting state at its actual selected target should report `resting`, not continue trying to walk and eventually report `path_blocked`. In the developer-directed dev case on `cde20004`, the log recorded one `IA_Interact`, later movement retries and a stuck failure. Post-action native state reported `IsResting=true`, `CurrentRestingAreaActor` equal to the requested shelter, `IsSleeping=false`, and `DIM_Resting`; endurance rose from 0 to 33 and then 54. The setup included a developer-controlled time change and a real shelter spell using carried runes. It was not an autonomous success. The first press preceded the observed resting state, but neither its exact transition time nor its cause was captured; probe getter names alone do not prove side-effect freedom. No claim of nighttime sleep follows from daytime rest.

The deployed episode captures `W.press_signals` before use but retains only `sleeping`, not `resting_in`; it recognizes same-target rest only for the separate initial leave-rest action. After verification times out it can re-approach, while its approach check rejects only a *different* resting actor, eventually failing the no-progress watchdog. This is a source-consistent explanation, not a complete trace of intermediate reads. Current `528ae99` is separately tested and published; R23 screen handling does not establish rest-transition causality.

## Approach

Reuse the existing interaction episode and the reflected `PlayerRestComponent.CurrentRestingAreaActor` observation. Introduce a small typed observation: **known nonresting**, **known resting with actor identity**, or **unknown/inconsistent**, with protected-read success tracked separately from values. The existing `W.resting_in` and `sense.time_of_day` helpers collapse missing component, failed reads and empty actor to nil, so nil alone cannot establish a nonresting baseline. The existing dev probe observed the `IsResting` getter returning boolean false and true, but its semantics, ABI and side effects beyond that observed use are not assumed: validate that the same supported read works safely in the current runtime; incompatible, failed, nonboolean or contradictory readings remain unknown. `IsSleeping` stays a separate fact and is never inferred from resting.

Immediately before an accepted real interact press, capture a known nonresting baseline, the actual detector-selected actor (including an accepted alternate), and stable pawn/arbiter ownership. A later known transition to resting at that **same selected actor**, with unchanged pawn and relevant ownership, can acknowledge the interaction as `resting`; report the observed actor and actual selected target, not an inferred unlock or night sleep. Check during the verify phase and again before retrying, declaring movement failure, or starting further approach work, so a late observation is not missed. If navigation is still active, stop it only through the existing owned-token path; never stop another owner. Baseline unknown, pre-existing rest, inconsistent getter/actor, a different actor, a changed pawn or lost ownership cannot prove the transition. Report the specific uncertainty or different-resting obstruction without suppressing a real failure; never press again while the observed state says resting. Preserve the existing same-actor-at-entry leave-rest behavior as a distinct request, including its own verified exit.

Pause and suspension invalidate the transaction's correlation; do no native calls under pause and do not credit an event while another owner controlled the pawn. On resume, do not silently re-baseline a prior press as a new success or send another press while already resting; surface a truthful uncertain/interrupted result unless a new explicitly accepted interaction is independently performed. Cancellation, timeout and failed reads retain R23's scoped screen cleanup and do not leak claims. These are evidence/ownership rules, not category, name, phrase or benchmark-specific mappings; no additional movement or monitoring framework and no new native ABI are proposed.

## Principles and acceptance

The model still chooses the action and target; code verifies observed game state and does not emulate rest or change inventory, health or saves. Preserve single voice, dev-only guards, one client/operator, world memory and frozen judges. No principle exception requested. A fresh-context review is required before implementation.

Offline fake-engine tests must cover known nonresting to same-target daytime resting with sleeping=false after one accepted press; a late transition before retry and during owned movement failure; explicit unknown and inconsistent getter/actor readings; pre-existing rest, other actor, accepted alternate detector, changed pawn, pause, cancel, suspension and another owner. Assert no duplicate press, no extra navigation after verified acknowledgment, no cancellation of another owner's movement, proper claim cleanup, and preservation of the existing rest-exit path. Then run focused regressions and the scheduled full gate. Live acceptance proceeds in the already approved order: fresh developer-driven observation with current version and game-state evidence, followed by a bounded Haiku autonomous case on the same reserved dev client, with resources, model calls and outcomes recorded. The earlier developer-directed observation is not the autonomous result.
