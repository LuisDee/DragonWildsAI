## Verdict
Mixed. Jumps and interruptions were mostly solved by real mechanism fixes. Copper (L2/V1) is a whack-a-mole: 0 of 14 L2 and 0 of 14 V1, after about 16 changes aimed at it. About 10 of those were "add one more true fact to a tool result", made one after another while the real block was in our own code: a note that told him to stop after a question (PROMISE_NOTE) and `ask_help` ending the job (agent.py:1660). A study found that (25 replays: more facts moved him 0 of 25). The facts were true and are fine to keep. The cost was ordering: rule 10 ("words tried once per cause, then structural") was never applied to tool-result text, so the cap never fired.
