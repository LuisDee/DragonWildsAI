# The board path (Director) before its trial: is it fit, and what will it cost (audit, 2026-11-17)

Question: the goal (`docs/GOAL-2026-11-17-long-jobs.md` section 1) decides the holder of a long ask by a trial of I3 (death) and I5 (brain restart) on each path. Before body time goes into it: is the board path (`DW_DIRECTOR=1`: `brain/board.py`, `brain/director.py`) fit to be trialled, and what does it cost?

Method: code read at HEAD `dd12d0d`; git history; `docs/PROGRESS.md`, `docs/DISCOVERIES.md`, `docs/HANDOVER-2026-10-31.md`; a scan of all 623 `logs/brain-live-*.jsonl` for sessions whose `session_start` has `director: true`; the quest run records `logs/evals/quest_runs.jsonl`; the offline unit tests. Nothing was run against the server, client or brain. No model calls.

## Verdict

**Fit to trial after 4 small steps** (2 in the harness, 2 in the brain; none near a native crash). The board already resumes a job after a brain restart (seen live by accident on 2026-11-02, below) and keeps a job through a death. What makes a trial unfair or broken today is around it: the harness never clears or saves the board, the per-trial spend cap stops the Director in about 4 minutes, a default-path fix from 2026-11-16 leaks the whole ask into every board step, and a restart is resumed in silence.

## 2. State of the code

**Tests:** `tests/test_board.py`, `tests/test_director*.py` (11 files), `tests/test_predicates.py`: **266 passed, 1 skipped** in 74 s (the skip: a cassette that stopped before a decision, `tests/test_director_cassettes.py:44`). None of them touch `_open_request`, `CONTINUE_FOR` or `STOP_FOR_NOTE` (grep), so they cannot show the drift in section 3.

**The tick** (`brain/director.py:1113-1158`). `Agent.pump` calls `director.tick()` last (`brain/agent.py:270-271`). `tick` returns at once if `DW_DIRECTOR=0` (`:1114`) or if already inside a tick (`:1116`; a board run that pumps re-enters here and returns). `_tick` serves proxied body reads, ingests the CLI inbox, collects skill learning, re-voices help, then runs up to 8 transitions (`MAX_TRANSITIONS`, `:74`), stopping when chat is waiting (`:1134-1136`). The board is written once per tick (`:1124`) and before each run (`:1580`).

**`_transition`** (`:1138-1158`): collect a finished decision; stop if paused; pick the task (`BoardOps.pick_current`, `brain/board.py:422-437`); **return while a decision is pending** (`:1144`); dispatch if a decision is awaited or `_needs_plan` (`:1149-1153`); wait if the agent is busy (a goal, a pending goal, situations) or chat is waiting (`:1154`, `AgentPort.busy` `:286-288`); a preempted task waits 30 s after the last chat command and while following (`:1156`, `_resume_ok` `:1173-1176`); else `_advance` (`:1476-1512`).

**`_needs_plan`** (`:1160-1165`): a specific job with no done test, or a job with no next step (an open job first yields to a queued specific one).

**`_dispatch`** (`:1203-1224`): if the budget (daily cap and hourly window, `:1185-1189`) cannot cover 5 requests, the task goes `waiting_budget` and a notice is voiced (`:1204-1215`). Else a fresh state read, `_pending = {task, key: plan_key, aw, started, snapshot_state}` and one worker call `_decide` over a deep-copied snapshot (`:1220-1224`). `_decide` (`:1276-1325`) runs `json_decision` with read-only research tools (recall, lookup, compare_gear, map_memory, skills_progress, learned_skills, proxied craft_plan / recipes / stations / buildables, look, lodestones; `:509-600`), 2 research rounds, 2 repairs, at most 5 requests (`:58-59`), each checked by the `Validator` (`:703-874`).

**`_collect_decision`** (`:1327-1362`): after 90 s the decision is abandoned and counted as a failure (`:1332-1337`). A result is applied only if the task still runs, is not paused, and `plan_key` is unchanged (`:1345`, `:1354-1357`); else `director_discarded`. `_apply` (`:1372-1438`) sets the job's done test once (a later one is ignored, `:1384-1390`), handles ask_help and done, keeps checkpoints, replaces the pending steps (`BoardOps.set_steps`, `brain/board.py:520-530`), adds at most one prerequisite job (`:1441`), and spends the progress budget if the decision came while the job had not moved (`:1437-1438`).

**The progress budget** (`brain/board.py:63`, `:702-723`; `brain/director.py:69`): 8 units a task. A run with no progress costs 1, a run ended by chat or an interrupt 0.25, a failed attempt 1, a decision while nothing moved 1, a loop pass with no gain 2. Any progress (the done test's progress or measures, the tallies, a step whose work grew) refills it to 0. Spent: the job is parked with a help question (`brain/director.py:1194-1199`, `:1769-1779`). Inside a step: two runs with no progress fail the attempt and ask for a replan (`:1654-1655`); 20 Controller calls in one attempt fail it (`:83`, `:1642-1645`); a run whose only failures are `busy` or `timeout` is retried once after 5 s (`:80-82`, `:1630-1638`).

**Preempt and resume.** A chat command that ran tools outside a board run preempts the board (`brain/agent.py:756-757` -> `brain/director.py:1088-1097` -> `brain/board.py:491-495`). The resume run carries `resumed: true` and `preempted_by` (who, what they said, which tools) and the model may call `pause_board` (`brain/director.py:1556-1559`, `:152-159`). `pause_board` / `resume_board` (`brain/board.py:462-489`); `resume` also revives the newest `waiting_help` task with a fresh budget.

**Park** (`brain/board.py:454-460`): `parked`, or `waiting_help` with a question; the task loses `current`, keeps its place, and is skipped by `pick_current` until `resume_board`.

**How a step reaches the Controller** (`brain/director.py:1540-1569`, `:1571-1655`): a `board_step` body event with `STEP_INSTRUCTIONS` (`:152-159`), the job text, the step (title, why, hints, escape), `step_done_when`, `step_progress`, the job's done test, checkpoints, `last_results`, a note when he reported done but the game disagrees, `resumed` / `preempted_by`, `just_completed`, `tell_player`, learned skills. It runs as `Agent.run_directed` (`brain/agent.py:893-932`), a body-initiated `_command` with that event. The plan's `first_call` runs first through the Controller's own gates (`brain/agent.py:934-969`). `brief()` (`brain/director.py:1037-1042`, `brain/board.py:736-765`) is the board as the Controller's context shows it: up to 6 live tasks, each with its done test and progress, the current step and its progress, tallies, a help question.

**How done is judged.** A step: its predicate on fresh state after the run (`brain/director.py:1611-1620`); a null result (can't be checked) fails the attempt and logs a perception gap (`:1690-1692`). The job: its done test, set once by the Director at the first plan, checked after each step that passes, and all ordered checkpoints done (`:1745-1748`). The model's `report_step(done)` is never done; it only adds a note to the next run (`:1624-1625`).

**The predicate vocabulary** (`brain/predicates.py:85-123`, registered at `:979-985`): `have`, `gained`, `lost`, `tally`, `on_ground`, `skill`, `skill_gain`, `equipped`, `gear_better`, `bag`, `station`, `structure`, `stored`, `elapsed`, `vital`, `spell`, `near`, `moved`, `killed`, `quest_complete`, `quest_step` (and the older `quest`), with `all`, `any`, `not`. More leaves can be registered (`:970-976`).

**Unchecked answer 1: what `plan_key` hashes** (`brain/board.py:497-506`). SHA-1 of: the task's status bucket (`done`, `stuck`, `cancelled`, `paused`, `parked`, `waiting_help` by name; every other status is `live`), its done test, `completion_mode`, `checkpoints`, the list of `(step id, step status)` for every step, the count of player notes, and whether the board is paused. Preemption, segments, tallies and window writes do not change it. **Yes, it goes stale when the actor merely starts or finishes a step:** `activate` sets `pending` to `active` (`brain/board.py:532-540`) and `finish_step` sets `done` or `failed` (`:664-672`), both in the hash. Today this never bites, because no step runs while a decision is pending (`brain/director.py:1144`) and a tick cannot run during a board run (`:1116`). It bites the moment the research note's item 3 (the actor runs while the planner thinks) is built: every step start or end would discard the pending decision (`:1354-1357`). The key would then have to hash only what the decision was about (for example a plan generation number bumped by `set_steps`, cancel, pause and a player note), not the live statuses.

**Unchecked answer 2: does the Controller re-read the brief inside one run?** **No, only at the run's start.** `brief()` goes in through `Controller.context()` (`brain/controller.py:628`), which builds only the first prompt (`decide`, `:717`). Follow-up turns (`next_turn`, `:893-947`) carry the tool results, `current_observation` (`:253`), the situation lines (`:703-715`, `:914-916`) and one note: no board. He can read it mid-run only by calling the `board` tool (`:987-988`). And the board cannot change mid-run anyway: the tick returns at `brain/director.py:1116` while a run is inside it, so a finished decision waits on the worker until the tick after the run. A run is at most 8 turns or 300 s (PROGRESS 2026-10-31). A revision therefore lands at the next step run's first prompt, which is pattern 7 of the research note, but only at run boundaries.

**A 20-minute job, estimated:**
- Board: 20 min x $0.034 to $0.095 = **$0.70 to $1.90**. Reasoning: run 5 is the realistic case for a job that meets deaths and blockers (a decision every 74 s on average); run 4 is a quiet one. Each `risk` step adds a `prepare` decision, and a `review` comes every 300 s while the window is more than half free (`brain/director.py:1531-1538`; uncapped, so always).
- Default: Haiku 20 x 3.1 x $0.001 = $0.06 to $0.10, plus 2 to 6 Planner calls (one or two goals with their replans) x $0.043 = $0.09 to $0.26: **$0.15 to $0.35**.
- So the board costs about 5 to 10 times more. Under the $10 guard: about 5 to 14 such jobs a day on the board, 30 or more on the default path, with nothing else running.
- **The trial itself:** I3 and I5 at k = 5 each, trials of about 5 to 10 minutes from a checkpoint: board about $0.2 to $0.9 a trial (a death adds a replan, $0.03 to $0.36), **about $3 to $9 for the set**; default about $0.5 to $2. The board arm wants a day of its own, or k = 3 first.
