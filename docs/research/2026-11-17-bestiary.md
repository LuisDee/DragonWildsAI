## What an entry holds

| Kind | Fields | Where each comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Creature | name, aliases (title, wiki redirects, game class stems), variants (label, power level, health), race, weak to, resistant to, immune to, aggressive, respawn, lives in, vaults and dungeons it is in (with count and power level), role (world, mini or vault boss), attacks (name and what it does), attack sentences, drops (item, amount, rate, note) | Monster infobox (also inside a Multi Infobox, with `versionN` and bullet lists); `Attacks`, `Attack styles`, `Combat mechanics` sections; `<Name>/Strategies` ("His main attacks are ..."); lead sentences after the first that name an attack; `DropsLine` rows; vault Enemies tables; the `Boss` page tables |
| Vault or dungeon | area, power level, number in the wiki's standard order, what opens it, enemies by power level with counts, hazards, boss (power level, health, weak to), notable enemies, chests, vault shards, recipes learned, notable loot, resources | Vault page (Infobox Scenery, lead, Enemies tables, Hazards, Resources, Notable loot, Recipes); `Dragonkin Vault` tables (order, power level, area, other monsters, recipes, rewards); `Boss` "Vault bosses"; `Chest (<vault>)` |
| Item sources (reverse) | item, and each source: creature drop (amount, rate), vault resource, recipe learned in a vault, vault loot, vault chests | The creature drops above, the `Boss` table's drop column, vault pages, `Dragonkin Vault` |

Every field has `src`: the wiki page ids it came from. Each entry has `text`: what the model reads, under 1,500 characters, facts only. Sentences and clauses that give advice or orders ("It is recommended...", "simply dodge out of the way", "then retaliate") are cut. A field the mirror lacks reads "not in the mirror".

Class stems come from names only: the dump's `/Gameplay/AI/<X>/` folders, `BP_AI_<X>_Character_C`, `DT_ATK_<X>`, and the combat memory's `Giant Rat:ATK_GiantRat_...` keys. 13 creatures have one (for example Giant Rat `GiantRat`, Goblin Runt `RuntGoblin`, Velgar `DragonVelgar`).

## The lookup with it (score only, no brain change)

A query is matched by name or alias: words lower case, plurals folded with `brain.knowledge._stem`, the longest name first, no overlap, at most 2 entries. A one-word name needs 4 letters or more. Matched entries go first. Then the old lookup's results follow, in order, while all texts stay within 3,000 characters. No match: the old result, unchanged.

Tuned half (`evals/lookup_enemies_cases.jsonl`, 15 cases):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before (lookup today) | 10/15 | 3/3 | 1/3 | 2/2 | 2/2 | 1/3 | 1/2 | 1,901 | 3,170 | 43 to 98 ms |

Size is the JSON the model reads, as the eval counts it. Every tuned query matched an entry. Loading the file takes about 60 ms once; a match takes about 1.5 ms.

The one entry-alone miss is `weak-plague-king`: the entry says "Immune to Poison"; the case accepts only the infobox's "immune: Poison". The old lookup's infobox text, kept after the entry, carries it. The text was not changed to fit the case.

Held-out half (`evals/lookup_enemies_heldout.jsonl`), scored once, at the end, through the eval's own one-line output: **HELD-OUT 15/15** (13/15 before, baseline doc). Its cases were not opened or read.

Caveat on the "Vertentis Kara chest" case: it is marked `answer_in_mirror: false`. It hits because the entry names the tables (skill tomes, weak potions) and the `Dragonkin Vault` shard line. The items inside the tables are still not known.

## Known limits

- The size grows: mean 1,901 to 2,919 chars. The 3,000 budget counts result texts only; the JSON around them adds the rest (max 3,850). Lower `TEXT_BUDGET` if size matters more than the old results.
- A creature name inside an item query matches the creature: in a probe of 17 item queries, 4 put a creature first ("cooked chicken", "wolf pelt", "sheep wool", "deer antler"). The old results still follow.
- Attacks are known for only 36 of 129 creatures. Most monster pages have no attack section.
- Vault "Opens" is read from lead sentences (locked, unlock, statues, effigies, mined, cut through). Four vaults have no such sentence.
