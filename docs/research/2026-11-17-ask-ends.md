# How a player's ask ends, and whether it was done (recorded live play, 1 to 17 Nov)

Offline log measurement for `docs/GOAL-2026-11-17-long-jobs.md` section 1. Nothing was run against the game or a model.
Tool: `tools/ask_ends.py` (`report`, `show ASK_ID`). Tests: `tests/test_ask_ends.py` (15, on samples cut from real logs).
Scan: every `logs/brain-live-*.jsonl` from 2026-11-01, files modified in the 30 minutes before the scan left out (scan at 2026-11-17 17:51Z).

## What the logs cannot say

- **The current code has not met a player.** Era 2 holds 181 asks: 180 quest-harness lines and one gate line. Every one of the 176 player-typed asks is from Nov 1, era 1. How `_open_request` behaves with Luis is unmeasured.
- **197 asks are unknown** (26 %). 165 are Luis's or Zezima's lines with no harness verdict; 81 of all unknowns ended by being replaced, and 69 were talk only. Free asks are judged mostly by gates and the ladder, whose lines are short jobs.
- **The two weak rules.** "Repeated within 2 minutes" (4 asks) counts a repeated "stop" as not done, though a repeat may mean the line was not heard. "His last line says he could not" (8 asks) is a word list; it does not read meaning.
- **Done is the harness's done.** A gate's done bar includes the method (the goblin killed with no runes spent is not done). A quest's done time is the run id (UTC) plus the step's `t`; a done time more than 30 s after the end counts as "done later".
- **Run ended** (80 asks, era 1): the run's rows stop with no row saying why (an action left running, a call whose result came in another episode). These are inferred, and 41 are unknown.
- **Deaths and full bags** are seen only through failed calls and situation reviews. A death during which he called nothing is invisible.
- **Goals** come from `progress.jsonl` files joined by time. 17 goals were still open when a log ended.
- **His claim** ("Rune Mysteries done") is not judged, only the game's verdict. A count of "done said while not done" needs a model or a predicate.
- **Not measured here:** the seconds he waits for the Planner and how often a plan arrived late. `tools/think_act.py` (another agent) measures time per ask.

## What the numbers support in the research note's "Decision" list

|---|---|---|

| 5. The planner called by events (a stall among them) | Supported | After a wrong close he made no body call in 54 of 81 cases. In 5 of the 9 era-2 asks the harness ended open and not done, he had stood 50 s or more. Holding the ask without a driver does not move it. |
| 2. Death and a full bag as interruptions with a true message | Death: weak support. Full bag: none | Deaths touch 9 of 112 not-done asks (8 %), and 6 of them ended by his silent "after I respawn" stop, not by `max_replans`. A full bag ended no ask in 766. I4 is a constructed case. |
| 1. The ask on disk, read back at start | Not supported by recorded play | No restart mid-job outside the harness was recorded. The need is real for I5 and for L2's length, but the logs give no count. |
| 7. Intent before dispatch, reconciled after a restart | Not supported by recorded play | Same reason: no restart mid-job, no repeated side effect seen. |

**Against the goal doc's table.** It lists death and the full bag as the paths that end a job ("two re-plans, then the goal is stuck"). In recorded play the full bag never ended an ask, and deaths ended asks mostly through the model's own silent stop, outside any goal. The largest loss is the one its first row names: the ask closes on any turn with no call, and nothing reads the game.
